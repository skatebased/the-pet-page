Typography

The face's own kern table is always on. Above it runs a true optical pass measured from the rendered glyphs: every display pair is rasterised, the gap between the facing ink profiles is compared with the face's own stem rhythm, and the difference is applied as a per-pair correction. It works on any face — including your licensed one — and never touches body copy.
