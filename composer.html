“Mars Is Closer Than You Think” Ad Campaign Launched Here

By Leland SchwartzEditor, The Middleburg Page

When we heard that the candy and pet food company — which makes Milky Ways and Mars bars for us, IAMS and Pedigree for our dogs and Temptations for our cats — wanted to make everyone aware of how local it was, we thought ‘what better way than through the local press?’

So we asked the company if it would run a year-long test to see if us local news editors could share their message wide enough to be a serious complement to their own social media efforts.

Can local news organizations help drive national brands and recapture a piece of what kept community newspapers alive for decades? That is the question. If this is the first you’ve heard of the Mars “Closer Than You Think” campaign, then we’re onto something.

What we’re thinking is to bring you two sets of stories over the year juxtaposed to Mars’ own messages. The first about rescues; how they work and how you can help, through our colleagues at the . The second describing how Mars is a thought leader in responsible pet ownership and animal adoption as well as how it makes its petfood and the science behind it.
